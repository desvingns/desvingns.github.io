Chop Chop Chef Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026 · Русская версия

This Privacy Policy explains how Chop Chop Chef (the "game") handles information. Chop Chop Chef is a mobile cooking and time-management game, for one player or two players in co-op, published by Skyward Atelier and developed by Kirill Shavrin. The game has no sign-in with a name, email address, phone number or social account.

Developer and contact

Developer: Kirill Shavrin (Skyward Atelier)
Email: desvingns@gmail.com
Telegram: @ShavrinK

Who the game is for

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete the related data we can identify.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, stars, coins, upgrades, outfits), settings (such as language, sound and controls), the nickname you type for co-op, and the state of purchases are stored locally in the game's private storage on your device. The game also generates a random installation identifier. It is not linked to your name, email or any account.

Co-op play

Same device

Two players on one device share the screen. Nothing is sent over the network.

Local Wi-Fi

To find a room, the game sends and listens for small broadcast messages inside your local network and connects directly to the other device. Match data (inputs and game state) travels only between the two devices on that network and is not sent to our servers. The other player can see the nickname you typed and your device's local network address.

Online (Unity Gaming Services)

Online co-op ("Play with a friend" and "Find a partner") is optional. Before your first online match the game shows a notice and asks you to accept it. Online co-op uses Unity Gaming Services, provided by Unity Technologies: Authentication (an anonymous player identifier, created per installation, with no name, email or phone number), Lobby (a room code and room settings used to find a partner), Relay (a relay server that carries match data between the two players, which means that your IP address is processed by the relay service) and Remote Config (a switch that lets us turn online play off). During an online match the game sends your inputs and the game state to the other player through Relay. The nickname you typed is shown to your partner only; it is not stored by us. There is no chat and no free text other than your nickname. Please do not use your real full name or other personal information as a nickname. Unity's own terms and privacy policy apply to these services.

Information sent over the network

Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

If you allow it in the consent dialog (you can change this at any time in Settings), the game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played in aggregate, for example which levels are played, how far players progress, and which screens and features are used. Firebase Analytics records usage events together with a Firebase installation identifier and technical device and app context (such as device model, operating system version, language and app version). On Android it may also read the device's advertising ID. We have turned off Google signals in Google Analytics, and event data is retained for 14 months. Google's own privacy policy and retention rules also apply. If you decline, no analytics events are sent.

Crash and error reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If you allow it together with analytics, then when the game crashes or hits a serious error, Firebase Crashlytics may send a crash report with a stack trace, technical device and app information (such as device model, operating system version and app version) and the installation identifier, so we can find and fix the problem.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads only. An ad is shown only if you deliberately choose to watch one in exchange for a reward, for example to double the coins you earned after a shift. There are no banner, interstitial or app-open ads. When an ad is requested, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your device's advertising ID and technical device and request context, and may use them to choose and measure the ad. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested. On iOS, the game asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using the advertising identifier for ads. Google's own advertising policies apply.

Purchases (Google Play and Apple App Store)

The game offers optional in-app purchases. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your card, bank or payment details. The game receives from the store only the purchase state needed to deliver what you bought (product identifier and purchase result). Refunds, cancellation and payment history are handled in your Google Play or Apple account. If you reinstall the game or switch devices, use the "Restore purchases" option where it is offered.

Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell your information. Information is processed by the service providers described above (Google LLC for Firebase and AdMob, Unity Technologies for Unity Gaming Services, and Google Play or Apple for purchases) under their own terms and privacy policies. These providers may process data in countries other than your own.

Retention and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until you clear the game's storage or uninstall the game. Firebase Analytics event data is retained for 14 months. Crash reports are retained according to Firebase Crashlytics' retention rules. The anonymous Unity player identifier and lobby data are kept by Unity under its retention rules; lobbies expire automatically shortly after a match.

You can ask us to delete data we hold about you by writing to desvingns@gmail.com. Most data is not linked to an account, so we can identify it only from details you can provide, such as the approximate time and your device model. To stop analytics and ad personalization on your device, you can decline consent in the game, and reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your device settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or elsewhere), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email above.

Security

Game data on your device is stored in the game's private storage. Data is sent to our service providers over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the game's data practices or the services it uses change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
